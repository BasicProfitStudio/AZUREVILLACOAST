The Azure Coast experience

We started Azure Coast Villas with a simple idea: a holiday house on the Aegean should feel like your own home, not a hotel room with a pool.

So we keep the collection small. Every villa is furnished with the same care — natural materials, good beds, kitchens you will actually cook in, and outdoor spaces arranged around the light and the view.

Before you arrive, we help you plan as much or as little as you like. Once you are here, we are a message away, and otherwise we leave you in peace.

Days shaped by the sea, the light and the pace you choose.

Bodrum and its coastline

Bodrum sits at the tip of a peninsula on Türkiye’s southern Aegean coast. Its whitewashed town curves around two bays beneath the medieval Castle of St Peter, and beyond it the peninsula opens into a string of coves, fishing villages and hillsides of olive and pine.

  • Beaches and bays

    From lively town beaches to small coves that are easiest to reach by boat.

  • Restaurants

    Meze and fresh fish by the water, village kitchens and evening tables overlooking the sunset.

  • Marinas

    Bodrum and Yalıkavak marinas, with gulet trips, sailing and day boats along the coast.

  • Coastal villages

    Gümüşlük, Türkbükü and Gündoğan each have their own character and pace.
